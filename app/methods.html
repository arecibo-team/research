Method and validation

The problem

NASA's MODIS sensors (Terra since November 2000, Aqua since July 2002) give the longest record of active fires. VIIRS (S-NPP since January 2012, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) sees far more, because its pixels are 375 m instead of 1 km. Stitching the two records invents a jump in 2012, and MODIS science data is planned to end in 2027. Ember Ledger expresses the whole record in one unit so that the sensor no longer matters.

1. Cleaning

  • Keep presumed vegetation fires (type = 0). Drop MODIS detections with confidence below 30 and VIIRS low-confidence detections.
  • Static heat sources. Early MODIS years carry no flag for industry and gas flares. We build a mask from every location VIIRS ever flags as static land or offshore (types 2 and 3), buffered by one 0.01° cell ( cells), and apply it to all sensors and years.

2. A common unit: fire cell-days

Detections are snapped to a fixed 0.02° grid (about 2 km) and each cell counts at most once per day. This removes most of the "one fire becomes many small pixels" effect before any statistics: a fire that VIIRS splits into ten 375 m pixels and MODIS sees as two 1 km pixels is one cell-day for both.

3. Harmonization

  • Reference sensor: VIIRS S-NPP only. Mixing NOAA-20 into the reference created a second break in 2018 and raised the validation error from about 4% to about 28%.
  • Per-month ratio of VIIRS to MODIS cell-days, fitted on the full overlap years . Small summer stubble fires and winter fires are missed more by MODIS, so the ratio varies by month:
  • Terra-only months (before Aqua, July 2002) are first scaled by a per-month (Terra + Aqua) / Terra factor fitted on 2003–2011.
  • From 20 January 2012 the series is observed VIIRS; before that it is modelled from MODIS. The app marks the difference everywhere.

4. Validation

Leave-one-year-out on the overlap: for each year, fit the ratios on the other years, predict that year's VIIRS total from MODIS, compare. Mean absolute error on yearly totals for Romania: .

The app repeats this check for every selected area and shows it as a trust badge and as the error band on modelled years. Small areas with few fires get lower trust.

5. What the app computes

  • Is this normal? The selected month against the same month in every other year: rank, ratio to the median, and the 10th–90th percentile range. A month still in progress is compared day for day (1–14 October against 1–14 October of other years).
  • Trend: Mann-Kendall test and Sen's slope on yearly totals of complete years.
  • Season window: days of the year when 10%, 50% and 90% of the year's activity is reached.
  • Recurring burn sites: number of years 2012–2025 in which each 2 km cell had a fire (VIIRS). Backtest: cells picked from 2012–2019 only, share of 2020–2025 fire days they capture, and lift over picking cells at random. For Romania, cells burning in at least 5 of 8 years are 5% of burned cells and caught 18% of later fire days (3.5×).
  • Weekly workload and county outlook: per week of the year, median and 90th percentile of harmonized activity over the last 10 complete years; counties ranked by the median for the next 6 weeks. This is a planning aid built from fire history, not a dispatch or resource-optimization system.
  • Fire events: VIIRS S-NPP detections within about 750 m on the same or the next day are grouped into one fire (union-find). Footprint is the number of distinct 375 m pixels × 0.14 km², an approximation, not a burned-area map.

Limits

  • Ratios are fitted for all of Romania by month, not per land cover or region; the local trust badge shows where this works less well.
  • Satellites pass at fixed times (about 10:30 and 13:30 local, plus night passes); short fires between passes are missed. Counts are a lower bound.
  • NRT data for the current year has no static-source type flag; the archive-based mask is used instead.
  • MODIS files mix product versions (6.03, 6.2, 61.03). We have not tested whether this matters.
  • Terra and Aqua orbits drifted in recent years, which may shift overpass times; the validation error stays small through 2022.

Adding the current year

FIRMS publishes country archives only for complete years. To add the current year, request a free MAP_KEY from FIRMS and run:

FIRMS_MAP_KEY=… python3 app/pipeline/fetch_current.py --raw RAW_DIR --counties ro_adm1.geojson
python3 app/pipeline/build.py --raw RAW_DIR --counties ro_adm1.geojson

The app then shows the partial year automatically, compares the month in progress day for day, and keeps trends and baselines on complete years.
